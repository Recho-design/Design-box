# Creative variants craft rules

Disciplines for generating orthogonal design directions, exploring structural alternatives, and translating references without imitation.
> Opt in via `od.craft.requires: [creative-variants]`.

---

## CREATIVE-01: Orthogonal variants contract

When generating multiple layout directions or concept proposals (e.g. 3 candidate drafts during concept exploration), proposals must diverge along **orthogonal structural axes**, not cosmetic surface tweaks.

### The four orthogonal dimensions

Every set of variations must select from these four independent dimensions:

| Dimension | Axis spectrum | Contrast choices |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Composition** | Geometric armature | Centered symmetry vs Asymmetric diagonal vs Split / Segmentation vs Broken grid vs Four-corner anchor. |
| **2. Visual gravity** | Primary visual anchor | Large photographic hero vs Giant typographic lockup vs Abstract graphic/data symbol. |
| **3. Information density** | Spatial economy | Ultra-sparse editorial (<20% text) vs Balanced modular cards (40–60%) vs High-density data grid (>70%). |
| **4. Emotional tone** | Brand stance | Quiet clinical restraint vs Raw brutalist tension vs Warm conversational vs Kinetic urgency. |

### Variant validation rules

1. **One-sentence visual hypothesis.** Each proposed variant must lead with an explicit hypothesis stating its structural rationale:
   > *"Hypothesis: High-contrast typography paired with an asymmetric diagonal split will drive immediate conversion by leading the eye directly to the terminal checkout CTA."*
2. **Minimum two structural divergences.** Any two candidate directions must differ across **at least two** of the four orthogonal dimensions above.
3. **Strict ban on pseudo-variants.** It is a failure to present "variants" that share the same wireframe while only swapping:
   - Primary accent color (e.g. blue button vs green button).
   - Corner radius tokens (e.g. `rounded-md` vs `rounded-full`).
   - Generic background gradients (e.g. purple-to-blue vs orange-to-pink).
   - Icon or placeholder image swaps within identical card grids.

## CREATIVE-02: Reference translation contract

Reference designs and templates provide principles and structural mechanisms, never verbatim layout replicas.

### The three-step translation protocol

When working from an upstream template, visual reference, or competitor benchmark:

1. **Extract** — identify the underlying mechanism: what structural truth makes it succeed (e.g. radical negative space, dramatic scale jump, strict 3-weight hierarchy).
2. **Transpose** — map the mechanism onto the project's orthogonal dimensions; change >= 3 major design decisions.
3. **Disclose** — document the lineage: state explicitly what was borrowed and what was mutated.

### Mandatory three-decision mutation rule

When adapting a reference, you must change **at least 3 major design decisions**:
- Visual gravity anchor (e.g. shift from image-led to typography-led).
- Structural composition armature (e.g. shift from centered symmetry to asymmetric split).
- Density and layout cadence (e.g. shift from single-column longform to modular multi-track grid).
- Palette and tonal posture (e.g. shift from saturated dark tech to light warm editorial).

### Mandatory attribution statement

Always provide a concise disclosure note in the design rationale:
> *"Reference lineage: Borrowed the radical 80% whitespace framing and microscopic metadata typography from Swiss poster traditions; altered the layout from static single-column to an asymmetric split, inverted the color scheme to dark mode, and replaced static captions with interactive data counters."*

## Creative trigger catalog (SCAMPER adaptation)

When candidate proposals feel repetitive or ideas stall, apply one trigger from each group to force structural mutations:

### 1. Morphological transformations (Shape & Scale)
- **Radical scale jump**: Magnify a secondary micro-detail into a 90vw background watermark, or shrink the primary subject to a 12% jewel.
- **Invert gravity**: Move the headline from the top header down to the bottom baseline; anchor navigation at the bottom edge.
- **Disrupt alignment**: Break one element out of the established CSS grid track by `-24px` margin to create an intentional focal puncture.

### 2. Spatial & context shifts
- **Enclose in a container**: Wrap an open layout into an archival file card, passport stamp, or terminal HUD frame.
- **Cross-axis intersection**: Introduce an orthogonal 90° vertical text spine into an exclusively horizontal reading plane.
- **Negative space inversion**: Swap positive figure and negative ground; let dark void become the active storytelling element.

### 3. Density & material mutations
- **Deconstruct into modular chips**: Break a continuous prose paragraph into 4 atomic data pill tokens with monospaced labels.
- **Halftone / Wireframe transparency**: Strip solid image fills down to an architectural wireframe or mono line-art silhouette.
- **High-density metadata stripe**: Attach coordinates, revision hashes, and timestamps directly below the display headline to inject tactical authenticity.
